# Simplest Ganapathi Homa - Action Manual

## Guru Dhyanam
`guru brahmA gurur vishNuhu guru dEvO mahESvaraha guru sAkshAt para brahmA tasmai SrI guravE namaha`

## Ganapathi hyanam <br>
`SuklAmbaradharam vishNum SaSi varNam chaturbhujam` <br>
`prasanna vadanam dhyAyEt sarva vighnOpa SAntayE` <br>
<br>
`agajAnana padmArkam gajAnanam aharniSam` <br>
`anEkadantam bhaktAnAm Ekadantam upAsmahE` <br>

## Achamanam <br>
Take a spoon of water in your right hand three times and drink each time: <br>

    aim Atma tatvAya svAhA
    klIm vidyA tatvAya svAhA
    sauh Siva tatvAya svAhA

 PrANAyAmam

Do 3 cycles of breathing by reciting the gAyatrI mantra mentally:

    Breathe in (pUrakam): 1 time
    Hold (kumbhakam): 2 times
    Release (rEchakam): 1 time
    Hold (bahir kumbhakam): 1 time

# Simplest Ganapathi Homa - Action Manual

## Phase 1: Setup & Pre-Fire

| Action / Mantra | Count / Frequency |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sit facing East. Pour water into your right palm 3 times and drink it:<br>1. `om kesavaya svaha`<br>2. `om narayanaya svaha`<br>3. `om madhavaya svaha` | **1 time each** (3 sips total) |
| Place water in your right palm, state your intent, and drop water over finger-tips. | **1 time** |
| Place copra pieces/twigs in the fire pit, light them, and say:<br>`om bhurbhuvassuvarom` | **1 time** |
| Offer drops of ghee in fire while saying:<br>`om bhurbhuvassuvah svaha` | **8 times** |

---

## Phase 2: Initial Offerings in Fire (Ghee / Oil)
*(For each of the following, offer a drop of ghee into the fire)*

| Mantra | Count |
| :--- | :--- |
| `om prajapataye svaha` | **1 time** |
| `om indraya svaha` | **1 time** |
| `om agnaye svaha` | **1 time** |
| `om somaya svaha` | **1 time** |
| `om bhurbhuvassuvah svaha` | **1 time** |
| `om gam ganapataye namah svaha` | **8 times** |
| `om vam varunaya namah svaha` | **1 time** |

---

## Phase 3: Services to Main Deity
*(Offer the respective items in the fire, or a drop of ghee in their absence)*

| Action / Offering | Mantra | Count |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Welcome deity into Holy Fire | `atra agaccha| avahito bhava|` | **1 time** |
| Offer sandalwood powder | `om lam prthivyatmane namah` | **1 time** |
| Offer a flower / dried flower powder | `om ham akasatmane namah` | **1 time** |
| Offer incense (light stick near homa kunda) | `om yam vayvatmane namah` | **1 time** |
| Show lamp to homa kunda | `om ram agnyatmane namah` | **1 time** |
| Offer a raisin / fruit piece / cooked food | `om vam jalatmane namah` | **1 time** |

---

## Phase 4: Main Ganapathi Offerings
*(Offer ghee, sesame seeds, and/or small pieces of dry coconut/nuts with each mantra)*

| Mantra | Count |
| :--- | :--- |
| `om gam ganapataye namah svaha` | **As many times as you can** (e.g., 8, 28, 108 times) |
| `om srim hrim klim glauw gam ganapataye varavarada sarvajanam me vasamanaya svaha` | **As many times as you wish** |
| `om gananam tva ganapatiy havamahe kavin kavinamupamasravastamam| jyestharajam brahmanam brahmanaspata a nah srtvannutibhissida sadanam|| svaha|` | **Optional** (if comfortable with swara) |

---

## Phase 5: Closing Offerings
*(Offer a drop of ghee for each)*

| Mantra | Count |
| :--- | :--- |
| `om bhuh agnaye svaha` | **1 time** |
| `om bhuvah vayave svaha` | **1 time** |
| `om suvah suryaya svaha` | **1 time** |
| `om bhurbhuvassuvah svaha` | **1 time** |
| `om vishnave svaha` | **1 time** |
| `om rudraya svaha` | **1 time** |
| Offer food/raisins/fruit pieces to ethereal associates outside the fire | **6 pieces** (`om parsadebhyo namah`) |

---

## Phase 6: Poornahuti (Complete Offering)

| Action / Mantra | Count |
| :--- | :--- |
| Offer a big copra piece, nut, or big drop of ghee:<br>`om gam ganapataye namah svaha` | **1 time** |
| Offer a drop of ghee:<br>`om agnaye saptavate svaha` | **1 time** |

---

## Phase 7: Meditation & Conclusion

| Mantra | Count / Duration |
| :--- | :--- |
| `om gam ganapataye namah` | **As long as you want** (meditate while chanting mentally or softly) |
| `om santih santih santih` | **1 time** (at the very end) |
